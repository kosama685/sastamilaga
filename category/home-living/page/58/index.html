Cash on delivery, explained

You do not pay anything to order. The rider brings the parcel, you open it, and you check it against the listing. Only then do you hand over the cash.

If the item is wrong, damaged or simply not what the photos promised, refuse the parcel. The order is cancelled and you owe nothing. That is the whole point of COD.
