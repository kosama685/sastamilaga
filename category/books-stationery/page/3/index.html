BUYING GUIDE

How to choose books & stationery

Start with what you will actually use this month. In books & stationery the price spread is wide — Rs 116 at the entry level and Rs 6,377 for the largest sets — and the cheap end is usually the right test buy. Check the size, weight and material in the specification table on each product page.

Items we hold locally are dispatched within 24 hours and reach most Pakistani cities in 2–5 working days. Items marked Global shipping come direct from a supplier warehouse, cost less, and take 10–25 days. Both are clearly labelled so a fast delivery is never a surprise.

PAYING SAFELY

Cash on delivery, explained

You do not pay anything to order. The rider brings the parcel, you open it, and you check it against the listing. Only then do you hand over the cash.

If the item is wrong, damaged or simply not what the photos promised, refuse the parcel. The order is cancelled and you owe nothing. That is the whole point of COD.

2–5 working days inside Pakistan with Leopards, PostEx, M&P or TCS. Gulf deliveries take 4–8 days. Supplier-direct items marked Global shipping take 10–25 days and are prepaid only.
