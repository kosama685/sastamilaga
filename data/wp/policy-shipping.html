Sasta Milaga delivers across Pakistan and worldwide. How an order ships depends on the collection the item belongs to: our Pakistan collection (local stock with cash on delivery) or our global collection (shipped from international partner warehouses to customers around the world). Each product page shows which collection it belongs to, and checkout confirms the delivery options and charges for your address before you pay.

Where we deliver

Browse items that ship to your country in the global shop. If checkout shows no delivery option for your address, that item cannot currently be sent there.

Pakistan collection: delivery within Pakistan

  • Cash on delivery is available on eligible local items.
  • Local delivery charge: Rs 250. Orders above Rs 5,000 qualify for free local delivery.
  • Usual delivery time: 2–4 working days in major cities and 3–6 working days in other areas after order confirmation.
  • Holidays, weather, address problems and courier operations can cause delays.

Global collection: worldwide delivery

  • Global items are prepaid, including deliveries to Pakistan. Cash on delivery is not available for them.
  • At checkout you choose an available shipping service. Each option shows its charge and an estimated delivery window for your full address; the product price does not include shipping.
  • Orders are processed after payment is confirmed. Delivery estimates are not guaranteed dispatch or arrival dates.
  • Express services (such as DHL) and standard tracked services are offered where available. Some remote destinations are served only by postal services, which may show limited or no tracking.
  • Remote-area surcharges, where they apply, are included in the quote shown at checkout.
  • Items from different warehouses can arrive in separate parcels.

Customs, import duties and taxes

International parcels can be subject to import duties, VAT/GST and customs handling fees set by the destination country. Unless checkout shows these amounts as included, they are not part of your payment to us and may be collected from the recipient by customs or the courier. Customs clearance can take longer in some destinations, including parts of Africa, South Asia and Latin America. Please check your country's import rules before ordering, and contact us before paying if you are unsure.

Address and delivery details

Enter a complete address with city, state or province, postal code and a phone number the courier can reach. Check it carefully before paying: a parcel sent to an incomplete or incorrect address may be returned or lost, and re-delivery can cost extra.

Tracking your order

Tracking appears in your orders and on the Track your order page once the courier provides it. A paid or processing order is not yet a dispatch confirmation. If tracking stops updating for an extended period, contact us with your order number.

Shipping questions

Do you deliver worldwide?

Yes. Items in our global collection ship to customers worldwide, including the USA, UK, Europe, the Gulf, Africa, Australia and New Zealand. Items in our Pakistan collection are delivered within Pakistan only.

How long does international delivery take?

It depends on the shipping service and your address. Checkout shows an estimated delivery window for each available service before you pay; express services are faster than standard and postal services.

Will I pay customs duties?

Possibly. Import duties and taxes are set by your country. Unless checkout shows them as included, the recipient may be asked to pay them on delivery.

Can I pay cash on delivery outside Pakistan?

No. Cash on delivery is available only for eligible Pakistan-collection items delivered in Pakistan. Global items are prepaid.

Can I order for someone in Pakistan from abroad?

Yes. Set your delivery country to Pakistan and enter the recipient's Pakistani address; local items and cash on delivery then become available.
