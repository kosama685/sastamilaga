Pakistan collection returns

  • Free return delivery for items that arrive damaged or where the wrong item was sent.
  • For other accepted returns sent by mail, the return delivery fee is Rs 250.
  • You can also return an eligible item in person at Sector 5-M, North Karachi, Karachi 75850, Sindh, Pakistan. Contact support first with your order number; do not leave an item without confirmation from our team.
  • Keep the item unused and in its original packaging where possible.

Global collection returns (international orders)

  • Damaged, defective or wrong item: report it within 7 days of delivery with photos and video. After review we arrange a replacement or a refund, at no extra cost to you.
  • Item not received: if tracking shows no delivery 30 days after the latest estimated delivery date, contact us and we will investigate with the carrier and arrange a replacement or refund if the parcel is lost.
  • Personalised or custom-made items cannot be returned or exchanged unless they have a quality defect.
  • Change of mind: where your local law gives you a right to cancel an online purchase (for example 14 days in the UK and EU), you can use it: tell us within that period and return the item unused; the return shipping cost is yours unless the item was faulty.
  • Always contact us before sending anything back. Do not send items to a warehouse or to the address printed on the packaging; we will give you the correct return address, and parcels sent without authorisation may not be accepted.
  • Wrong or incomplete addresses entered at checkout, refused deliveries and unpaid import duties are not eligible for a free reshipment.

Refunds

  • Pakistan collection returned by mail: refund within 7–10 working days after we receive the item, to an agreed bank account, JazzCash or EasyPaisa.
  • Accepted in-store returns: refund processing within 7 days.
  • Prepaid and international orders: refunded to the original payment method once approved; your bank or payment provider may take extra days to show it.
  • We will tell you when a request is received, approved and completed; a refund is never described as completed before it is processed.

How to request help

Message your regional support team or use the contact page with your order number, the item, the delivery date and the issue. Never share an account password, card security code or one-time code.

Returns questions

How long do I have to report a problem?

7 days from delivery for damaged, defective or wrong items, with photos and a video. Lost parcels can be reported 30 days after the latest estimated delivery date.

Do I pay for return shipping?

Not when the item is damaged, defective or wrong. For change-of-mind returns, the return shipping cost is yours (Rs 250 for mailed Pakistan returns).

Can I return an international order?

Yes, for damaged, defective, wrong or lost items, and where your local law gives a right to cancel. Contact us first so we can give you the correct return address.
