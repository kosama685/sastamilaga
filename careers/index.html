        Live TV questions

        Is Sasta Milaga Live TV free?

        Yes. Every channel listed is a free public stream offered by its broadcaster. There is no subscription, sign-up or app to install.

        Which Pakistani news channels can I watch live?

        Pakistani news channels appear in the News category when their broadcasters provide a public stream. Availability changes, so the list only shows channels that have been reviewed and are online.

        Can I watch PSL, cricket or football matches live here?

        Only when the rights holder streams the match free to the public. Most major tournaments are licensed to pay-TV and official apps, and we do not list unauthorised streams.

        Does it work outside Pakistan, in the UK, USA, UAE or Saudi Arabia?

        The player works in any modern browser worldwide. Some broadcasters limit their streams to certain countries, so a channel can be unavailable where you are.

        Why does a channel not play?

        The broadcaster may be offline, changing its stream, or limiting it by country. Try again later or choose another channel. Channels are checked regularly and broken streams are removed.

        Do you host the videos?

        No. Sasta Milaga only links to streams published by their owners. Rights holders can ask for a channel to be removed through our contact page.

        What happens if browser playback fails?

        The player retries recoverable network and media errors. It can try the secure relay when a direct stream fails. If the source remains unavailable or uses an unsupported format, choose Try browser again, another channel, or Open VLC app. VLC must be installed; playlists download only when you choose Download playlist.

        Can I use these channels on a smart TV or in Kodi?

        Yes. Download one of the M3U playlists on this page and add it to VLC, Kodi, IPTV Smarters, TiviMate or your TV's IPTV app.

        Which devices are supported?

        Android and iPhone browsers, tablets, Windows and Mac computers and smart TV browsers. Use the full-screen button for the best picture.
