Privacy policy

We collect the minimum needed to deliver a parcel: your name, phone, address and order contents. Nothing is sold.

What we collect

  • Name, phone number, city and delivery address.
  • Order contents and the WhatsApp conversation needed to confirm it.
  • Basic, aggregated analytics about which pages are visited.

What we do not collect

No card numbers, no CVV, no bank credentials. There is no account system and no password stored on this site. When prepaid payment is needed we send a secure payment link and the payment provider handles the details.

How it is used

To confirm the order, print a courier label, respond to support questions and handle returns. Couriers receive only the details needed to deliver.

Retention

Order records are kept as long as needed for warranty, returns and accounting. You can ask for your contact details to be deleted once an order is closed and the return window has passed.

Cookies and local storage

This store uses browser local storage for your basket and currency preference. It is not shared with advertisers. There are no third-party tracking pixels on this build unless explicitly enabled by the store owner.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion by messaging WhatsApp. We respond within 7 working days.

Questions about this page

Do you sell my phone number?

No. It is used for order confirmation and courier contact only.

Is this site secure?

It is served over HTTPS as static files with no login, no database and no card forms, which removes the usual ecommerce attack surface.

Do you use advertising cookies?

Not by default in this static build. If pixels are added later, this page will list them before they run.

Still need a human?

Message us on WhatsApp — order status, sizes, returns, bulk pricing or sourcing a product that is not listed.
