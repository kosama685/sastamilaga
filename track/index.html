STATUS STAGES

What each stage means

  • Pending confirmation — order received, stock being checked
  • Confirmed — you replied YES on WhatsApp
  • Packed — picked, photographed, labelled
  • Shipped — handed to the courier
  • Out for delivery — the rider is on the way
  • Delivered — cash collected
  • Returned / RTO — refused or undeliverable

Tracking questions

How do I get a tracking number?

We send it on WhatsApp as soon as the courier scans the parcel — usually within 24 hours of confirmation for local stock.

The tracking page shows no movement. What now?

Courier scans can lag by a day. If there is no movement for 48 hours, message +966 51 045 1995 and we chase it the same day.

Can I change the address after dispatch?

Usually yes, until the parcel reaches the destination city. Send the new address on WhatsApp and we request a re-route with the courier.
