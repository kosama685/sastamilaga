Questions about shopping in Pakistan

How much is delivery in Pakistan?

Delivery is Rs 250 per order and free for orders of Rs 5,000 or more.

Can I pay cash on delivery?

Yes, for local products delivered in Pakistan. Global supplier items use a prepaid checkout instead.

How do I place an order?

Add items to your basket on the shop and check out, or fill in the order check below and send it on WhatsApp.
