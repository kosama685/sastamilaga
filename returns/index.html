Returns & refunds

14 days from delivery, unused and in its packaging. Faulty or wrongly sent items are returned at our cost.

What can be returned

  • Unused items in original packaging within 14 days of delivery.
  • Wrong item, wrong size or wrong colour sent by us — free return, we pay the courier.
  • Items damaged in transit — report within 48 hours with photos.

What cannot

  • Used, washed or altered clothing and footwear.
  • Opened cosmetics, skincare, oral-care and personal-hygiene items.
  • Underwear, swimwear and pierced jewellery.
  • Items damaged after delivery by the customer.

How to start a return

  1. Message WhatsApp with the order number and a photo.
  2. We approve or ask one clarifying question within 1 working day.
  3. Pack the item and hand it to the courier we book, or drop it at the address on the label.
  4. Once it reaches us, the refund is approved within 2 working days.

Refund timing

Bank transfer, JazzCash or Easypaisa within 5–7 working days of approval. For COD orders the refund goes to the account you nominate — we cannot reverse cash, so we ask for details once.

Refused parcels

If you refuse a parcel at the door, nothing is charged and the order closes. Repeated refusals of confirmed orders may lead to prepaid-only ordering on that phone number.

Questions about this page

Who pays return shipping?

We do when the item is faulty, damaged or not what you ordered. You do for a change of mind, unless the item is defective.

Can I exchange instead of refund?

Yes, if the replacement is in stock. Exchanges follow the same 14-day window.

Do you refund the delivery fee?

Yes when the return is our fault. For change-of-mind returns the original delivery fee is not refunded.

Still need a human?

Message us on WhatsApp — order status, sizes, returns, bulk pricing or sourcing a product that is not listed.
