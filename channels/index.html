Channel questions

Which countries have cash on delivery?

Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Egypt, Morocco, India, Bangladesh, Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Greece, Romania, Bulgaria, Poland, Spain, Italy, Portugal, most of Latin America and parts of West Africa.

Where is cash on delivery not available?

Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the USA, Canada, Germany, France, South Korea, Singapore and Japan. Those markets are prepaid only, and checkout says so instead of hiding it.

Why does COD depend on the country?

Cash is collected by local couriers, not by us. A country with no COD courier network cannot have COD orders, so we publish the real coverage rather than accepting orders we cannot fulfil.

Can I change my delivery country after ordering?

Message +966 51 045 1995 before dispatch. If the destination is outside COD coverage we may need to switch the order to prepaid.
