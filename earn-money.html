Safety, Privacy & Account Quality

Do not use fake identities, duplicate accounts, bots, automated searches, deceptive referrals, paid review manipulation, or VPN/location tricks where prohibited. Never pay a “registration fee” to unlock ordinary work. Read privacy policies for bandwidth-sharing and passive-data apps. For crypto, treat token earnings as speculative rather than guaranteed income.

Educational directory only. It does not guarantee platform eligibility, earnings, payment or legal/tax treatment.

Legitimacy & Review Methodology

✅ Established platform: recognizable official presence; this does not guarantee income, availability or a positive user experience.

🟡 Verify current status: platform may be usable, but current country support, payout rules, task volume and reputation should be checked.

⚠️ Caution / verify: speculative, legacy, unusually low-value or higher-risk services. These are not presented as primary-income recommendations.

Review links: every platform entry has a direct URL where available plus a one-click search for recent Trustpilot/Reddit discussions. Official platform terms are authoritative for eligibility and payment; independent reviews are for user experience and red flags.
