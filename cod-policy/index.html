Cash on delivery policy

How COD works, where it is available, its limits, and what happens when a parcel is refused.

The rule

You order without paying. The courier brings the parcel. You open it, check the item against the listing, and only then hand over the cash. If it is not right, refuse it and pay nothing.

Limits

  • COD is available from Rs 199 to Rs 15,000 per order inside Pakistan.
  • Above Rs 15,000 we ask for a small advance or full prepayment — high-value parcels attract fraud on both sides.
  • COD fee: none. Delivery is free over Rs 2,500, otherwise Rs 199.

Where COD is available

Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Egypt, Morocco, India, Bangladesh, Thailand, the Philippines, Indonesia, Vietnam, Malaysia, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Greece, Romania, Bulgaria, Poland, Spain, Italy, Portugal, most of Latin America and parts of West Africa. Prepaid only: Australia, New Zealand, the UK, the USA, Canada, Germany, France, South Korea, Singapore and Japan.

Confirmation

Every COD order is confirmed on WhatsApp before dispatch. Unconfirmed orders are held for 24 hours, then cancelled. This protects stock for customers who actually want it.

Refused and undeliverable parcels

A refused parcel is returned to us at our cost the first time. Repeated refusals on the same phone number, or obviously fake addresses, may result in prepaid-only ordering for that number.

Fraud checks we run

  • Duplicate phone and address matching.
  • Invalid or incomplete phone formats.
  • History of cancelled or returned orders.
  • High-value first orders flagged for confirmation.

Questions about this page

Do I pay anything at checkout?

No. Nothing is charged when you place a COD order. Cash changes hands only at your door.

Can I pay part cash and part online?

No. A COD order is settled in full with the courier. Use a prepaid order if you prefer to split payment methods.

Is there a COD fee?

No COD fee. You pay the product price plus delivery (free over Rs 2,500).
