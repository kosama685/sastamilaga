How to watch and find programme listings

Select a channel to open its browser player. Choose a source or let automatic failover try the next available mirror. If a browser cannot play the feed, use its supplied broadcaster link or choose an installed player such as VLC or MX Player.

Are all channels live and available everywhere?

No. Directory inclusion and a reachable playlist do not guarantee playback. Broadcasters control regional access, codecs and availability. Records without a public stream stay visible so coverage remains transparent.

Where can I find TV shows and today's schedule?

The player checks the channel's supplied programme guide and shows current and next programmes in your device timezone where listings exist. Missing guides are labeled; programme titles and schedules are never invented.

How many channels are listed under All TV channels: complete A–Z directory — page 57?

This page lists 40,293 channel records for All TV channels: complete A–Z directory — page 57. Each card shows the channel name, its language, how many sources are available and whether a public stream has been supplied.

Is a public stream supplied for every channel here?

No. Each card shows how many sources are available, and channels with no public stream are labelled "No public stream supplied". Broadcaster availability can change at any time, so a listed channel is not a promise of playback.

Where do the programme schedules come from?

Schedules appear only where supplied TV guide data exists for that channel. Where no guide data has been supplied, the channel page shows its sources and programme information instead of an invented schedule.

Who owns these channels and who runs this directory?

The channels belong to their broadcasters. Sasta Milaga publishes a directory of channel and programme information with the sources it has been given, and does not own, operate or license the channels themselves.
