How to watch and find programme listings

Select a channel to open its browser player. Choose a source or let automatic failover try the next available mirror. If a browser cannot play the feed, use its supplied broadcaster link or choose an installed player such as VLC or MX Player.

Are all channels live and available everywhere?

No. Directory inclusion and a reachable playlist do not guarantee playback. Broadcasters control regional access, codecs and availability. Records without a public stream stay visible so coverage remains transparent.

Where can I find TV shows and today's schedule?

The player checks the channel's supplied programme guide and shows current and next programmes in your device timezone where listings exist. Missing guides are labeled; programme titles and schedules are never invented.
