Customs, taxes and product rules for Serbia

Serbia is not in the EU, so parcels from abroad pass through Serbian customs. Customs duty and VAT can apply depending on the goods and their value, and the postal operator or courier will tell you what is due. These charges are not included in Sasta Milaga's price.

Some goods have labelling and conformity requirements in Serbia. Ask us about the specific item before you order it.

This is general guidance, not legal or tax advice. Rules change; check the official source for your country.

Questions about delivery to Serbia

Is customs duty included when I order to Serbia?

No. Serbia sits outside the EU customs area, so duty and VAT can be charged on delivery depending on the goods and their value. Sasta Milaga's displayed price is an item estimate before shipping, taxes and duties.

How do I know the total before I pay?

Sasta Milaga confirms the item option, quantity, shipping method and total with you before you pay. The price on a product page is an estimate in US dollars before shipping, taxes and duties.

Can I return an item ordered for delivery to Serbia?

Return eligibility depends on the item and the reason. Read the returns and refunds page before you order and ask us about a specific item if you are unsure.

Is this page available in my language?

Not yet. Sasta Milaga publishes English and Urdu pages. Other-language pages will be added only after they are properly translated and reviewed.

Do you list every product for Serbia?

No. The global collection is a supplier catalogue. Whether a given item can be shipped to Serbia depends on the item, its option and the available shipping methods, so it is checked for your order.
