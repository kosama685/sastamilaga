Customs, taxes and product rules for UK

Goods delivered to the UK from abroad can attract UK VAT and, for some goods, customs duty. These are not part of Sasta Milaga's displayed price. Check HMRC's current rules for goods you import and allow for any charge the carrier collects.

Some goods, such as electricals, toys and cosmetics, must carry UK product-safety markings. Ask us about the specific item before you order it.

This is general guidance, not legal or tax advice. Rules change; check the official source for your country.

Questions about delivery to UK

Are UK VAT and customs duty included in the price?

No. The price shown on Sasta Milaga is the item price estimate before shipping, taxes and duties. UK VAT or duty may be charged on delivery depending on the goods and their value, so check HMRC guidance for imported goods.

How do I know the total before I pay?

Sasta Milaga confirms the item option, quantity, shipping method and total with you before you pay. The price on a product page is an estimate in US dollars before shipping, taxes and duties.

Can I return an item ordered for delivery to the United Kingdom?

Return eligibility depends on the item and the reason. Read the returns and refunds page before you order and ask us about a specific item if you are unsure.

Is this page available in my language?

Not yet. Sasta Milaga publishes English and Urdu pages. Other-language pages will be added only after they are properly translated and reviewed.

Do you list every product for the United Kingdom?

No. The global collection is a supplier catalogue. Whether a given item can be shipped to the United Kingdom depends on the item, its option and the available shipping methods, so it is checked for your order.
