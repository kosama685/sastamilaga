Customs, taxes and product rules for Germany

Germany is part of the EU single market. Parcels sent from outside the EU can be charged import VAT and, depending on the goods and their value, customs duty. Those charges are not included in the price you see on Sasta Milaga; the carrier or customs office tells you what is due.

Electrical goods, toys and cosmetics sold in the EU must meet EU product-safety rules, such as CE marking where it applies. Ask us about a specific item before you order it.

This is general guidance, not legal or tax advice. Rules change; check the official source for your country.

Questions about delivery to Germany

Will I pay import VAT on an order delivered to Germany?

It can happen. Goods shipped from outside the EU are generally subject to import VAT, and customs duty can apply to some goods. Sasta Milaga's price does not include these charges, so allow for them and check current German customs guidance before you order.

How do I know the total before I pay?

Sasta Milaga confirms the item option, quantity, shipping method and total with you before you pay. The price on a product page is an estimate in US dollars before shipping, taxes and duties.

Can I return an item ordered for delivery to Germany?

Return eligibility depends on the item and the reason. Read the returns and refunds page before you order and ask us about a specific item if you are unsure.

Is this page available in my language?

Not yet. Sasta Milaga publishes English and Urdu pages. Other-language pages will be added only after they are properly translated and reviewed.

Do you list every product for Germany?

No. The global collection is a supplier catalogue. Whether a given item can be shipped to Germany depends on the item, its option and the available shipping methods, so it is checked for your order.
