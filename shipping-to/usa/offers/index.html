Shopping questions

Is this an active sale or an event ticket?

These pages provide shopping collections and preparation guides. Only a separately labelled promotion with published terms is an active offer. No admission, booking or official event affiliation is implied.

How should I choose a product for an occasion?

Check its intended use, size, materials, colour and exact variant. A decorative image is not proof that a product matches a local tradition. Confirm suitability with the recipient or host.

Can an item arrive before my celebration?

Ask for a destination-specific shipping quote and availability check before paying. An occasion date is not a guaranteed arrival date. Allow time for preparation, carrier handling and customs where applicable.
