Shipping & delivery

Free delivery over Rs 2,500 inside Pakistan, otherwise a flat Rs 199. Everything else is priced by the courier before you confirm.

Pakistan

2–5 working days to Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar, Quetta and most other cities. Couriers: Leopards, PostEx, M&P and TCS. Delivery is free above Rs 2,500 and Rs 199 below it. Remote areas may add one working day.

Gulf

Saudi Arabia 4–8 working days (SMSA, Aramex, Naqel); UAE 3–7 days (Aramex, COD Network); Kuwait, Qatar, Oman and Bahrain 5–10 days. Cash on delivery is available in major cities.

Rest of the world

7–18 working days depending on the destination and the courier. Prepaid where no COD network exists. Import duty and VAT charged at the border are separate and collected by the courier.

Local stock vs global shipping

Local stock is picked and packed by hand and dispatched within 24 hours of confirmation. Global shipping items come from supplier warehouses and take 10–25 days. The product page and the parcel label both state which one you bought.

Packaging

Items are packed to survive the courier network, with the invoice inside and the return address on the label. If a parcel arrives visibly damaged, refuse it — that is a courier claim, not yours.

Questions about this page

Do you deliver on Sundays?

Couriers do not run regular Sunday deliveries in Pakistan. Orders placed on Sunday are dispatched on Monday.

Can I collect from a courier office?

Yes, most Pakistani couriers allow office pickup. Ask on WhatsApp and we will book that instead of door delivery.

Is delivery free for every product?

Free above Rs 2,500 inside Pakistan. Below that it is Rs 199. International rates are shown at checkout.
