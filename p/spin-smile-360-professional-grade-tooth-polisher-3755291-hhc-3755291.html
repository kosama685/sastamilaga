Product details

The device is a battery-operated, handheld polisher that uses a high-speed rotating tip (spinning at 18,000 RPM) to gently lift stains from tooth enamel. It is intended to give you a "dentist-clean" feeling and a brighter smile with just a few minutes of use.​Key Features​High-Speed Rotation: Spins at 18,000 RPM to effectively remove tough stains caused by coffee, tea, wine, and tobacco.​Built-in LED Light: Helps you see exactly where you are polishing, even in the back of the mouth.​Safe for Sensitive Teeth: Designed with a gentle touch in mind to avoid discomfort for those with tooth sensitivity.​Interchangeable Tips: Comes with 5 polishing cups (small rubber tips) that can be replaced as they wear out.​Travel-Friendly: Includes a protective travel cover, making it easy to carry in a bag or suitcase.​How to Use It​Attach Tip: Place one of the rubber polishing cups onto the top of the device.​Apply Paste: Put a small amount of your regular toothpaste or a whitening gel inside the cup.​Polish: Place the cup against your tooth and turn the device on. Move it gently over the surface of each tooth for a few seconds.​Do Not Press Hard: The motor is designed to stop if you press too hard to prevent damage to your enamel. A light touch is best.
