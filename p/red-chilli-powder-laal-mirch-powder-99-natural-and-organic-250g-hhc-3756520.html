Product details

Red chilli powder, or Lal Mirch powder, is a common spice made from finely ground, dried red chili peppers, known for its vibrant red color, bold, fiery flavor, and versatile use in global cuisines. It adds heat and complexity to dishes like curries, sauces, and marinades, with variations in heat level and color, such as the mild, colorful Kashmiri chili powder. The spice is rich in capsaicin, the active compound that provides its characteristic heat, and also offers potential health benefits, including aiding digestion, boosting metabolism, and providing pain relief.Key CharacteristicsOrigin: Made from dried and ground red chili peppers.Appearance: Ranges from bright to dark red.Flavor: Hot, spicy, pungent, and can have a smoky quality.Active Compound: Contains capsaicin, which provides its heat and potential health benefits.Culinary UsesGlobal Staple: A fundamental spice in Indian, Mexican, Mediterranean, and other cuisines.Dishes: Used to add heat and flavor to curries, stews, sauces, soups, marinades, and grilled meats.Spice Blends: A key component in spice mixes such as garam masala.Health Benefits
