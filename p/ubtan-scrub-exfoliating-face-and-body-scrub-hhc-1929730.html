Product details

Introduction: Ubtan Scrub is a modern skincare product inspired by this ancient beauty ritual. It is a natural exfoliating scrub made from a blend of finely ground herbs, grains, and other natural ingredients, such as turmeric, sandalwood, chickpea flour, and rose water, that work together to gently slough off dead skin cells, unclog pores, and improve skin texture and tone. The Ubtan Scrub can be used on the face or body to brighten and even out the skin tone, reduce the appearance of blemishes, and leave the skin feeling smooth and refreshed. It is typically applied to damp skin and massaged in a circular motion before being rinsed off with warm water. The Ubtan Scrub is suitable for most skin types, but it's important to perform a patch test before using it, especially if you have sensitive or reactive skin. How To Use: Here are some general steps on how to use Ubtan Scrub: Take a small amount of Ubtan Scrub and mix it with water or rose water to create a paste. You can also add some milk or yogurt for added hydration and nourishment. Gently apply the paste to your skin in a circular motion, massaging it in for 1-2 minutes. Let the scrub sit on your skin for a few minutes, allowing the natural ingredients to work their magic. Rinse off the scrub with warm water, making sure to remove all of the paste. Pat your skin dry with a clean towel. Ubtan Scrub is typically used 1-2 times a week, depending on your skin type and concerns.
