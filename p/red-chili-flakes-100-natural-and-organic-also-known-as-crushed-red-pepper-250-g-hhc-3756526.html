Product details

Red chili flakes are dried, crushed red chili peppers, known for adding a spicy kick and vibrant color to dishes. They are a versatile spice used in various cuisines, from Italian to Mexican, and can be sprinkled on top of food or added to sauces. The flakes include both the flesh and seeds of the chili peppers, contributing to their characteristic heat and flavor.Key characteristics:Appearance:Red chili flakes are small, flat pieces of dried chili pepper, ranging in color from bright red to reddish-brown.Flavor:They offer a spicy, sometimes smoky, flavor, with the intensity varying depending on the type of chili pepper used.Texture:The flakes are typically dry and crunchy, adding a textural element to dishes.Versatility:Red chili flakes can be used as a condiment, a flavoring agent in sauces and marinades, or as a garnish.Culinary Uses:They are a staple in Italian, Mexican, and Tex-Mex cuisines, and are also popular in Indian dishes.
