The Non-Contact AC Voltage Alert Device is an essential safety tool for every household, electrician, and DIY enthusiast. Designed for ease of use, this tester pen senses the steady state electrostatic field produced by AC voltage through insulation without requiring metal contact. The integrated white LED flashlight allows you to work conveniently in dark areas like basements or behind appliances.
