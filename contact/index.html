Contact us

One channel, answered by a person: WhatsApp. Send your order number, a product link or a photo and we will reply with a real answer.

WhatsApp (fastest)

+966 51 045 1995 — orders, stock checks, size advice, returns, tracking, bulk pricing.

Before you message

  • For an existing order, include the order number and the phone number used at checkout.
  • For a return, attach a photo of the item and the parcel label.
  • For a product that is not listed, send a photo or a link — we will quote price, delivery time and COD eligibility.

Response times

Sunday to Friday, 10:00–22:00 (PKT). Messages outside those hours are answered the next morning. Order confirmations are prioritised over general questions.

Returns address

The return address is printed on the parcel label and included in your WhatsApp order thread, so a return never has to be guessed.

Business and wholesale

Resellers, offices and bulk buyers: message the same number with “BULK” and the quantity. We quote tiered pricing and can consolidate one shipment.

Questions about this page

Do you have a call centre?

No call centre. WhatsApp keeps a written record of every order, which is better for both sides than a phone call nobody can prove.

Can I visit a shop?

We operate as an online store with courier fulfilment; there is no walk-in showroom for the full catalogue.

How do I cancel an order?

Message the order number before dispatch. Cancellation is free at any point before the parcel leaves.

Still need a human?

Message us on WhatsApp — order status, sizes, returns, bulk pricing or sourcing a product that is not listed.
