# Product SEO audit

**Date:** 2026-11-20. **Method:** Read-only SQL COUNT queries over SSH on the live WordPress/WooCommerce database (tables wp_posts, wp_postmeta, wp_term_taxonomy); no rows were modified.

## Scope and what "the catalogue" is
- **Local WooCommerce catalogue (measured here):** 11,763 published products. This is the real number; the earlier 400,000 and 600,000 figures describe the CJ supplier snapshot, not the shop.
- **CJ supplier snapshot (measured separately):** 451,812 rows in 572 categories, dated 2026-11-05. Not imported into WooCommerce and not sellable through native WooCommerce checkout (see the payment matrix).

## CJ snapshot measurements (reports/catalog-snapshot-metrics.json)
451,812 rows; no row missing image, price, name or SKU; 0 duplicate IDs; 2 duplicate SKUs; 19,741 rows repeat an earlier name; 3,380 names under 15 characters; maximum snapshot price 999,999 (an unreviewed outlier). The snapshot has no descriptions, attributes, stock or verified retail prices, so it cannot support product pages that deserve indexing.

## What exists and what does not
- **Exists (verified to exist, content not reviewed here):** per-SKU draft SEO and category-link plans generated by another session (see `reports/categories-keywords-interlinking.csv` and the commits "Build per-SKU draft semantic SEO..." and "Generate full 451812-record draft SEO..."); static local product pages with internal links.
- **Not built by this work:** a product optimization pipeline for the WooCommerce catalogue (unique titles, clean URLs, specification-based descriptions, product-specific FAQs, image alt text, schema validation), batch checkpoints and rollback. Doing it responsibly needs reviewed specifications per product; generating text from titles alone would create the thin and duplicated content this audit warns against.
- **Highest-value fixes the numbers point to:** (1) 2,889 products without an image, (2) 506 duplicate title groups, (3) 2,175 products without a SKU, (4) 19 unpriced published products.
