SEO, answer content and regional experience

The raw homepage title, description and social metadata now describe global and local shopping, instead of defining the whole site as Pakistan-only. The server-rendered homepage canonical remains https://sastamilaga.com/. No country-query duplicate pages, invented local addresses or unsupported delivery guarantees were added.

Natural category terms connect shopping intent with fashion, beauty, electronics and home essentials. Homepage ordering, shipping, returns and payment answers are factual and destination-aware. Crawlable shipping, returns and ordering links connect these answers to the existing support pages. Homepage structured data points to real shopping and service URLs. The AI-readable site introduction now describes the destination and display-currency model; all 41 canonical links across the two AI navigation documents passed validation.

Existing product/category URL structures, qualified supplier indexing, canonical normalization, retired-product responses and semantic internal-link generators remain in place. AEO/GEO work improves factual retrieval and clarity; it does not guarantee selection by an answer engine, geographic ranking or Google indexing.

Performance and checkout protection

No new production JavaScript or stylesheet request was added. Country behavior is integrated into the existing deferred market script. The hero reuses the global manifest already loaded by the homepage; no extra supplier shard or geolocation request was introduced. WooCommerce remains responsible for cart and checkout, and no WooCommerce product-page links were introduced.

The anonymous WooCommerce test selected Pakistan through the official CSRF-protected context endpoint, added one existing local item, verified cart persistence, opened checkout with billing fields and COD, then removed only its test item. PayPal and COD were both enabled in the backend. The existing CJ shipping integration returned seven shipping options for one mapped variant to Saudi Arabia. No order was placed, payment captured or supplier fulfillment started.
