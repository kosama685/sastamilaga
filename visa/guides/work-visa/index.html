Visa guide

Work visa: from job offer to departure, step by step

A work visa lets you take paid employment abroad, nearly always with a sponsoring employer. For Pakistani workers the process has two parts: the destination country's visa and work permit, and Pakistan's emigration clearance before you fly.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Get a genuine job offer

    From a real employer, with a written contract that states salary, hours, duties, accommodation and contract length. Verify the employer independently.

  2. 2

    Employer starts sponsorship

    Most countries need the employer to obtain a work permit, labour approval or sponsorship certificate before your visa.

  3. 3

    Prepare and attest documents

    Educational degrees are usually attested by HEC and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, and by the destination's embassy when required. Get a police character certificate.

  4. 4

    Medical examination

    Many countries require a medical test at an approved centre. Gulf countries use GCC-approved medical centres booked through the Wafid (formerly GAMCA) system.

  5. 5

    Apply for the visa

    Online, at the embassy or at the official visa centre, using the employer's approval.

  6. 6

    Pakistan emigration clearance

    Before departure, register and obtain protector clearance with the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment, usually through a licensed Overseas Employment Promoter.

  7. 7

    Arrive and complete residence formalities

    Get your residence card or permit, register as required and keep copies of your contract.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passport valid for at least the contract length plus six months
  • Signed employment contract or offer letter
  • Employer's work permit, labour approval or sponsorship reference
  • Attested degrees, diplomas and experience letters
  • Police character certificate
  • Medical fitness certificate from an approved centre
  • Photographs, CNIC copy and completed forms
  • Protector clearance from the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment (Pakistan)

Tips and warnings

  • Never pay for a job offer to an unlicensed agent. Licensed Overseas Employment Promoters are listed by the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment.
  • A real employer does not ask you to travel on a visit visa and "convert" it later.
  • Keep your passport. In most countries it is illegal for an employer to hold it.
  • Read the contract before you sign and keep a copy at home.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need protector clearance?

Pakistani workers going abroad for employment normally need registration and protector clearance with the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment before departure.

Can I find a job after arriving on a visit visa?

In most countries working on a visit visa is illegal. Look for jobs from Pakistan through licensed promoters or official job portals.

How long does a work visa take?

It depends on the employer's approvals, document attestation and medical checks. Plan for several weeks to a few months.
