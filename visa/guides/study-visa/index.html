Visa guide

Student visa: admission to arrival, step by step

A study visa lets you enrol in a recognised institution abroad. The key decisions are made on your admission, your ability to pay tuition and living costs, your English or local-language level and your genuine intention to study.

Ask our team Choose a country

Step by step

  1. 1

    Choose a recognised institution

    Check the institution is licensed to admit international students in that country.

  2. 2

    Meet the language requirement

    Take the test the institution and the visa rules accept, such as IELTS, PTE or TOEFL.

  3. 3

    Get your admission

    Receive the offer and any official acceptance document the visa needs.

  4. 4

    Prove funds

    Show tuition and living costs for the period the country requires, held in an acceptable way.

  5. 5

    Apply for the visa

    Online or at the official visa centre; give biometrics and attend an interview if asked.

  6. 6

    Prepare for arrival

    Arrange accommodation, health insurance and registration with the institution and local authorities.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passport
  • Offer or acceptance letter and fee receipt
  • Academic transcripts and degrees (attested where required)
  • Language test results
  • Bank statements, scholarship or sponsor letters
  • Statement of purpose explaining your study plan
  • Health insurance and medical certificate if required
  • Tuberculosis test where the country requires it

Tips and warnings

  • Explain clearly how the course fits your past studies and future plans in Pakistan or abroad.
  • Funds should be genuine and explainable; sudden large deposits raise questions.
  • Check work-while-studying rules; they are strict and limited in most countries.

Frequently asked questions

Can I work while studying?

Many countries allow limited part-time work during term. Rules and hours differ; follow your visa conditions exactly.

Can my family come with me?

Some countries allow dependants for certain courses only. Check the current rules before applying.
