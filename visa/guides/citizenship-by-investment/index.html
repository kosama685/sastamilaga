Citizenship by investment: how second-passport programmes work

Citizenship-by-investment programmes grant citizenship, and a passport, to people who make an approved economic contribution, usually a government fund donation or approved real estate. The best-known programmes are in the Caribbean. Due diligence is strict and rules change often.

Step by step

  1. 1

    Confirm dual citizenship is possible for you

    Pakistan allows dual nationality with a list of countries; check the current position with the Directorate General of Immigration & Passports and NADRA before applying.

  2. 2

    Compare programmes

    Look at visa-free access, total cost, processing route, residence rules and family inclusion. Check status on the official government site.

  3. 3

    Use an authorised agent

    Most programmes accept applications only through licensed agents. Verify the agent on the government list.

  4. 4

    Pass due diligence

    Background checks on you and your family, including the source of your funds.

  5. 5

    Make the contribution or investment

    Normally after approval in principle, following the official sequence.

  6. 6

    Take the oath and get your passport

    Some programmes require an interview or a short visit.

Documents you will usually need

  • Passports, birth and marriage certificates for every applicant
  • Police clearance certificates
  • Detailed source-of-funds and source-of-wealth evidence
  • Medical certificates
  • Professional reference and bank reference letters

Tips and warnings

  • Visa-free access lists for some second passports have been reduced by other countries. Check the current list before you invest.
  • Beware of discounted offers below the official minimum; they can lead to revocation.
  • An EU court ruling in April 2025 found Malta's investor citizenship scheme incompatible with EU law; EU citizenship by investment is not a realistic route today.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to live in the country?

Most Caribbean programmes do not require long residence, but some require a short visit or an interview.

Will I lose my Pakistani citizenship?

Pakistan permits dual nationality with specific countries. Check the current list with the Directorate General of Immigration & Passports before you apply.
