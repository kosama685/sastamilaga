How to get a Singapore visa

For Singapore you apply online for the e-Visa, or through the embassy for longer stays and other purposes. Work, study and residence always need the matching visa from Singapore's authorities.

Singapore visit visa: step by step

  1. 1

    Apply online for the e-Visa

    Use Singapore's official e-Visa portal only. Third-party sites charge extra and some are scams.

  2. 2

    Check your passport

    At least six months of validity beyond your trip and blank pages are commonly required.

  3. 3

    Prepare the documents

    Follow the checklist below; every document should support the same purpose, budget and return plan.

  4. 4

    Submit and pay the fee

    Apply and pay on the official portal, then upload or present your documents.

  5. 5

    Biometrics or interview

    Bring originals and answer consistently with your documents.

  6. 6

    Check the visa and travel

    Verify names, dates, entries and allowed stay before you fly.

Documents checklist for a Singapore visit visa

  • Passport valid for six months or more, with blank pages, plus copies of old passports and visas
  • Recent photographs to the destination's size and background rules
  • Completed application form and fee receipt
  • Bank statements (often the last six months) with a balance that fits the trip
  • Employment letter with leave approval, or business registration and tax returns if self-employed
  • Hotel booking or the host's invitation letter and a copy of their ID or residence permit
  • Return or onward flight reservation and a day-by-day itinerary
  • Travel insurance for the whole stay (recommended, mandatory in some countries)
  • Polio vaccination certificate, required for travellers leaving Pakistan

The embassy may ask for more. Full guide: visit visa guide.

Avoid refusal

  • Explain any large recent deposit in your bank account; unexplained funds are a common reason for refusal.
  • Show ties to Pakistan: a job, a business, property, family or studies you will return to.
  • Never submit fake bookings, bank letters or invitations. A finding of deception can lead to a long ban.
  • A previous refusal must be declared. Fix the reason before you apply again.

Singapore work visa

To work in Singapore you normally need a job offer and an employer-arranged work permit, followed by a work visa. Check the official rules and verify the employer.

From Pakistan, register and obtain protector clearance with the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment before departure, ideally through a licensed Overseas Employment Promoter. Bureau of Emigration

Frequently asked questions

Do Pakistanis need a visa for Singapore?

Pakistani passport holders can apply online for an electronic visa (e-Visa) before travel for Singapore, according to the last published data (January 2025). Rules change: confirm with the official portal before you travel.

How do I apply for a Singapore visit visa from Pakistan?

Apply online for the e-Visa, check your passport, prepare bank statements, an employment or business letter, bookings and travel insurance, then submit online on the official portal and attend biometrics or an interview if asked.

Can I get a Singapore work visa from Pakistan?

To work in Singapore you normally need a job offer and an employer-arranged work permit, followed by a work visa. Check the official rules and verify the employer. Pakistani workers also need protector clearance from the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment before departure.

Does Singapore offer residence or citizenship by investment?

We are not aware of an investment programme for Singapore. Residence usually follows work, study or family routes.

How long does a Singapore visa take?

Processing time depends on the visa type, the season and your documents. Apply as early as allowed and check the current estimate on the official portal.
