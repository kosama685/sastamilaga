Quick answer: do Pakistanis need a visa for Kenya?

Ordinary Pakistani passport holders visiting Kenya generally need an approved electronic travel authorisation (eTA) before departure, unless an official exemption applies. Check your circumstances on the official eligibility portal. Admission remains subject to immigration checks.

Kenya eTA and immigration permissions

For ordinary short visits, check eligibility and apply through the official Kenya eTA portal. An eTA authorises travel; immigration officers determine the permitted stay on arrival.

Work, study and longer-term residence require the appropriate permits or passes; an eTA alone is not permission to work or study.

Documents checklist for a Kenya visit visa

  • Passport valid for at least six months after planned arrival, with at least one blank page
  • Passport-type photo or selfie
  • Email address, telephone and contact information
  • Arrival and departure itinerary
  • Accommodation booking or host information
  • Supported payment method
  • Purpose-specific evidence when requested, such as business or family invitation and vaccination documentation

Check the current official Kenya eTA checklist for your purpose of travel. General guidance: visit visa guide.

Avoid refusal

  • Use the official application portal and keep your reference.
  • Enter accurate passport, itinerary and contact details.
  • Submit genuine supporting documents and respond to official requests.
  • Confirm approval and travel eligibility before departing.

Frequently asked questions

Do Pakistanis need a visa for Kenya?

Ordinary Pakistani passport holders visiting Kenya generally need an approved electronic travel authorisation (eTA) before departure, unless an official exemption applies. Check your circumstances on the official eligibility portal. Admission remains subject to immigration checks.

How do I apply for a Kenya visit visa from Pakistan?

Check eligibility, prepare the official eTA checklist, apply online and wait for approval before travel. Extra documents may depend on your reason for visiting.

Can I get a Kenya work visa from Pakistan?

Employment in Kenya requires the applicable work permit. Check the employment permit class and submit the required documents through the official eFNS portal. Verify the employer and the permit before working. Check the current Pakistani protector-clearance requirements with the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment before departure.

Does Kenya offer residence or citizenship by investment?

We are not aware of an investment programme for Kenya. Residence usually follows work, study or family routes.

How long does a Kenya visa take?

Processing time depends on the visa type, the season and your documents. Apply as early as allowed and check the current estimate on the official portal.
