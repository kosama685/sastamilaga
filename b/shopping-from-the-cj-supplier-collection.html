Shopping from the CJ supplier collection

Shopping guides 31 October 2026 · Sasta Milaga team

Choose your shopping destination before comparing products in Sasta Milaga’s global supplier collection. A displayed price in your preferred currency is an estimate; checkout confirms the payment currency and final total.

Choose the option you need

Check the product’s available options and quantity. Different options can have different prices, stock and shipping origins. Where a product video is available, use it alongside the written product details rather than assuming every option is identical.

Check delivery before paying

Delivery availability and charges depend on the selected option, quantity, origin and destination. A product appearing in the catalog does not guarantee that every shipping service can deliver it to your address. Review the services and delivery estimates shown at checkout.

Review your order

You pay Sasta Milaga at the store’s checkout. Sasta Milaga manages the supplier order; you do not need to send a separate supplier payment. Review your address, selected options, shipping service and final total before confirming your purchase.

Local products in Pakistan

Sasta Milaga Local local supplier products and their cash-on-delivery checkout are available for Pakistan destinations. Global supplier items use the supported prepaid checkout. Local and global items may require separate baskets.

Start with a category

Browse Books & Stationery, Tools & DIY or the global supplier collection. Catalog coverage grows as supplier data is discovered; displayed discovery counts describe the records found so far.
