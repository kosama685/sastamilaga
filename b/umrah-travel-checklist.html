Umrah travel checklist: documents, packing and planning

10 November 2026By Sasta Milaga travel team1 min read

Good preparation makes Umrah calmer and lets you focus on worship. Use this checklist a few weeks before you travel.

Documents

  • Passport valid for at least six months from your travel date, with blank pages.
  • Umrah or tourist visa approval, printed and saved on your phone.
  • Return flight ticket and hotel confirmations for Makkah and Madinah.
  • Vaccination records your airline or the Saudi authorities require at the time of travel.

Packing

  • Ihram sheets (men) and comfortable modest clothing (women), plus a spare set.
  • Comfortable sandals you can walk in for long distances, and a small bag for shoes at the mosque.
  • Unscented toiletries for the state of ihram, a small prayer mat and a refillable water bottle.
  • Power bank, universal adapter and any medicines in their original packaging.

Booking tips

Prices for flights and hotels near the Haram change with the season. Travelling outside school holidays and Ramadan is usually cheaper. Send your dates through our travel search and the team will reply on WhatsApp with options you can compare before paying.

Questions people ask

How early should I book Umrah?

Book flights and hotels as early as you can, especially for Ramadan and school holidays when prices rise.

What documents do I need for Umrah?

A valid passport, an approved visa, return tickets and hotel bookings. Check current health requirements with your airline before travel.
