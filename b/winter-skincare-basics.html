Winter skincare basics for dry weather

23 November 2026By Sasta Milaga team1 min read

Cold air and heaters dry out skin. A short, steady routine helps more than many products.

  1. Cleanse gently with lukewarm water and a mild, fragrance-free cleanser.
  2. Moisturise on damp skin so the cream holds in water. Thicker creams suit winter.
  3. Protect lips and hands with a balm and hand cream, especially after washing.
  4. Keep using sunscreen on sunny winter days.

If you have a skin condition, follow your doctor's advice before trying new products.

Questions people ask

Do I need sunscreen in winter?

Yes, on sunny days. Ultraviolet light still reaches your skin in winter.

Ready when you are

Keep skin comfortable in cold, dry weather with a gentle cleanser, moisturiser, lip care and sunscreen. A simple routine anyone can follow.
