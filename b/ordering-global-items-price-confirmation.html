Ordering global items: how price and shipping confirmation works

18 November 2026By Sasta Milaga team1 min read

The global shop lists products from our supplier network that can ship to many countries. Because stock and shipping change often, we confirm every global order with you before payment.

How it works

  1. Choose a product and note the estimated price, shown in US dollars.
  2. Send a request with the quantity, any size or colour, and your country and city. It opens WhatsApp with the details filled in.
  3. Get the confirmed total. We check stock and shipping to your address and send the final price.
  4. Pay securely by card or PayPal link once you are happy with the total.

You never pay before you have seen the final price including shipping.

Questions people ask

Why are global prices estimates?

Supplier prices and shipping change. We confirm the final total, including delivery to your address, before you pay.

Can I pay cash on delivery for global items?

No. Global items are prepaid after we confirm the total.
