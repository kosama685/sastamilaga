Kitchen organisation ideas for small homes

14 November 2026By Sasta Milaga team1 min read

A small kitchen works best when everything has a place you can reach. Start with the items you use every day.

Use the walls

Adhesive hooks and wall racks hold ladles, towels and lids without drilling, which suits rented homes.

Stack, don't spread

Clear stackable containers for rice, lentils and spices save shelf space and show when you're running low.

Divide the drawers

Drawer organisers stop cutlery and tools from piling up, so you can find them quickly while cooking.

Under the sink

A two-level rack under the sink doubles the space for cleaning supplies.

Questions people ask

How do I organise a kitchen without drilling?

Use adhesive hooks, over-the-door racks, stackable containers and drawer organisers.

Ready when you are

Make a small kitchen easier to use with racks, stackable containers, hooks and drawer organisers. Practical ideas that work in rented homes too.
