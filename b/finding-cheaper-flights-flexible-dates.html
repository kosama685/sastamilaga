Finding cheaper flights with flexible dates and routes

20 November 2026By Sasta Milaga travel team1 min read

Airfares depend on demand, so small changes to your plans can make a real difference.

  • Move your dates by a day or two. Midweek departures are often cheaper than weekends.
  • Compare nearby airports. For example Lahore and Islamabad, or Dubai and Sharjah.
  • Check one-way combinations. Two one-way tickets on different airlines can cost less than a return.
  • Avoid peak periods such as Eid, school holidays and Ramadan when you can.
  • Check baggage rules before you compare prices; a cheaper fare without luggage may cost more in the end.
