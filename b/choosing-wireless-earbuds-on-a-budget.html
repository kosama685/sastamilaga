Choosing wireless earbuds on a budget

12 November 2026By Sasta Milaga team1 min read

Budget earbuds have improved a lot. These points help you pick a pair that suits how you use them.

What to check

  • Fit: look for several ear-tip sizes in the box. A good seal improves bass and blocks noise.
  • Battery: compare playtime per charge and total playtime with the case.
  • Bluetooth version: 5.0 or newer usually connects faster and more steadily.
  • Noise cancelling (ANC): useful on buses and in busy places; it uses more battery.
  • Controls and calls: touch controls and a microphone on each bud make calls easier.

Watch before you buy

Many products on Sasta Milaga include a video. Watching it shows the real size of the case and how the controls work.

Questions people ask

Is ANC worth it on cheap earbuds?

If you commute or work in noisy places, yes. In quiet rooms a good fit matters more than ANC.

Which Bluetooth version should I choose?

Bluetooth 5.0 or newer for a steadier connection and better battery use.
