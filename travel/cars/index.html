How the price you see is built

Supplier cost in its own currency, converted at a stored FX rate, then our fee, then rounding up to a whole unit. On cars the fee is 20%. Partner rows are exempt: you pay the partner directly at their price and we take a commission, so adding a fee there would be dishonest.

If a fare cannot clear our minimum margin after payment processing, we hide it rather than show a price we cannot honour. Full detail: how pricing works.

Cars questions

What deposit do I need?
It depends on the supplier and the car class, usually a credit-card hold rather than a charge. The quote states the exact deposit and excess before you confirm.
Can I drive from Saudi Arabia to the UAE?
Some operators allow it with a cross-border permit and extra insurance. Tell us the route and we quote only the operators that permit it.
Is a Pakistani licence accepted?
In the UAE an international driving permit is normally required alongside your licence; in Pakistan your own licence applies. We confirm the exact document list for your booking.
