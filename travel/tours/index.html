How the price you see is built

Supplier cost in its own currency, converted at a stored FX rate, then our fee, then rounding up to a whole unit. On tours the fee is 20%. Partner rows are exempt: you pay the partner directly at their price and we take a commission, so adding a fee there would be dishonest.

If a fare cannot clear our minimum margin after payment processing, we hide it rather than show a price we cannot honour. Full detail: how pricing works.

Tours questions

Are tours instant confirmation?
On partner marketplaces, most are. Anything we quote manually is confirmed in writing before you pay, with the cancellation terms for that specific supplier.
Can you build a custom itinerary?
Yes. Send dates, cities, pace and interests on WhatsApp or through the request form and we return a day-by-day plan with a firm price.
Do you provide guides in Urdu?
In Makkah, Madinah, Dubai and Istanbul we can arrange Urdu and Arabic speaking guides. Tell us the language when you request the quote.
