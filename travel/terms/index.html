Terms of service

The rules of using this site: indicative pricing, who the seller is, supplier terms, and your responsibilities.

1. What this site is

A travel comparison and booking desk. Prices are indicative until a supplier confirms them in writing. Nothing on this site is a binding offer to sell at the displayed price.

2. Pricing

Our own fares and rates include a 20% service fee, applied before display. Partner prices are shown as-is and you pay the partner. We re-price before payment and show any change; you may decline for a full release of the authorisation.

3. Who sells your booking

For bookable and quote modes, we are the seller and the contract of carriage or accommodation is with the airline, hotel or supplier whose terms apply. For redirect mode, the contract is with the partner and their terms govern.

4. Supplier terms

Airline fare rules, hotel cancellation policies, rental excess and visa conditions are set by the supplier or authority, not by us. We show them before payment and record your acceptance.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Passenger names exactly as printed on the passport. Corrections may be impossible or chargeable.
  • Valid passports, visas and health documents for your nationality and route.
  • Arriving at the airport with the baggage and documents your fare allows.

6. Visas and Umrah

We do not issue visas. Visa decisions belong to the issuing authority. Umrah packages and visas are fulfilled by a Ministry of Hajj and Umrah licensed operator, and we act as an arranging agent for hotels, transport and guiding.

7. Liability

We are not liable for supplier failure, schedule changes, weather, strikes or government action, beyond passing on refunds we recover from the supplier. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

8. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms; the version in force is the one published when you book. Disputes are handled under the law of the jurisdiction where the business is registered.

Questions

Is the displayed price guaranteed?
No. It is indicative until a supplier confirms it. We re-price before charging and you can decline any increase.
Whose terms apply to my ticket?
The airline's fare rules and conditions of carriage, plus the supplier's terms. We show you the key rules before payment.
