Refund and cancellation policy

How refunds work for flights, hotels, tours, cars, Umrah and visas, who decides eligibility, and the timelines.

Who decides eligibility

The supplier. An airline's fare rule, a hotel's cancellation window or a tour operator's policy decides whether a refund is due, and how much. We do not have discretion to override it, and we will not pretend otherwise.

Flights

  • Before ticketing: full release of the authorisation, no charge.
  • After ticketing, refundable fare: airline refund minus any airline fee, processed when the airline pays us.
  • After ticketing, non-refundable fare: taxes and fees only, where the airline permits it.
  • Schedule change or cancellation by the airline: rebooking or refund per the airline's policy, and we pursue the refund for you.

Hotels and tours

Governed by the property or operator's cancellation window. Free-cancellation bookings refunded in full if cancelled before the deadline; later cancellations follow the supplier's policy, which we show before you pay.

Cars

Pay-at-pickup bookings cost nothing to cancel before pickup. Prepaid rates follow the rental company's policy, usually with a late-cancellation fee.

Umrah and visas

Hotel and transport components follow each supplier's terms. Government visa fees are non-refundable once the application is submitted, because the authority has already performed the assessment.

Timelines

Card refunds usually appear in 5–10 business days after approval, longer for international airlines. We send you the supplier's approval reference so you can track it. Our own service fee is refunded when the underlying booking is refunded in full.

How to request one

Message WhatsApp with your booking reference. We confirm the applicable rule in writing before anything is cancelled, so you always know the outcome before you commit.

Questions

Do I get your service fee back?
Yes when the underlying booking is refunded in full. Where a supplier keeps part of the fare, the fee is refunded in the same proportion.
How long does a flight refund take?
Typically 5–10 business days after the airline approves it, sometimes longer for international carriers. We send you the approval reference.
