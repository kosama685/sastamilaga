Straight answers to what people actually search

No filler. These are the questions behind the highest-intent travel searches we see.

cheap flights from karachi to dubai

Karachi to Dubai is a 2h 20m direct flight with flydubai, Emirates, Air Arabia and PIA on the route. Weekday departures and a 21-day advance purchase are consistently the cheapest, and the ±5 day calendar on the results page shows when a neighbouring date is cheaper.

umrah package from pakistan with hotel and visa

A complete Umrah package is four purchases: flights, Makkah hotel, Madinah hotel, and the visa through a licensed operator on Nusuk. We quote hotels, transfers and Ziyarat, and work with a licensed operator for the visa. Expect Ramadan's last ten nights to cost two to three times a regular month.

hotels near haram makkah walking distance

We quote the distance in metres and whether the route is walkable, rather than using the phrase 'near the Haram'. Clock Tower and Ajyad area hotels are the closest; the further into Kudai you go, the more you rely on the shuttle.

is it cheaper to book flights directly or through an agent

Usually the airline's own site and a good agent see the same fare, and the difference is the fee and the support. We show one 20% service fee inside the price, and we show partner prices with no fee at all, so you can compare honestly.

Why this is different from a normal OTA

Most travel sites either hide the markup inside a "total price" you cannot audit, or they scrape a competitor's page and show you a number nobody will honour. We do neither. Every price on this site comes from one of three places, and the row tells you which:

  • Our own fare or rate. Sourced from a supplier we are licensed to use, with a single 20% service fee applied before it reaches your screen. The fee is the same whether the fare is cheap or expensive.
  • A partner price. Booking.com, Agoda, Viator, DiscoverCars, Airalo and similar. We send you their official link, add nothing, and earn a commission from them. If they are cheaper than us, that is what you see.
  • Indicative (DEMO) data. Where no supplier key is connected yet, rows are clearly badged DEMO so the table shape is honest without pretending a live seat exists.

Frequently asked questions

How does this site make money?
Two ways. When we sell you a flight, stay or package ourselves, one 20% service fee is already inside the price you see. When the best price sits on a partner site, we show their price unchanged and earn a commission from the partner instead. You never pay a hidden fee in either case.
Do I need to create an account?
No. Search freely without registering. When you are ready, you send the trip to us on WhatsApp and we confirm the fare and send a secure payment link. There is no login wall and no account to manage.
Are the prices on this site live fares?
Partner rows open the partner site with that partner's live price. Our own fare rows are labelled DEMO until supplier keys are connected, and every price is indicative until our team confirms it in writing. We re-price before charging, and you can walk away at that point.
Which destinations do you cover?
Directly, we quote from Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad, Peshawar, Multan and Sialkot to the Gulf, Saudi Arabia, Turkey, Southeast Asia, the UK and North America, plus 24 destination guides. If a route is not listed, ask — we can almost always quote it.
Can I book Umrah or a visa here?
Umrah hotels, transfers and Ziyarat yes; the Umrah visa itself is processed by a licensed operator through Nusuk, because only a Ministry of Hajj and Umrah licensed company may sell those packages. Tourist visas are applied for on the official government portal, and we review your documents first.
What happens if a fare changes after I pay?
We re-price before capturing payment. If the supplier price has moved, we show you the new total and ask for your confirmation. If you decline, you are refunded in full, and our refund policy sets out the timeline in writing.
