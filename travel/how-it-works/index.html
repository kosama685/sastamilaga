How it works, including the pricing

Exactly how our prices are built: supplier cost, stored FX rate, one 20% service fee, rounding, and human confirmation before payment.

The pricing engine, in plain terms

One formula, applied on the server, never in your browser:

finalPrice = roundUp(supplierPrice × storedFxRate × (1 + 20/100) + fixedFee)

  1. Supplier cost. The net price the supplier quotes us, in their currency.
  2. Conversion. We store the FX rate and the timestamp used, so the same search cannot drift between pages.
  3. Markup. A flat 20% on our own inventory. Partner inventory is exempt, because you pay the partner.
  4. Rounding. Up to a whole unit, so the number you see is the number you pay.
  5. Margin guard. If the fee does not cover supplier fees and payment processing, we hide the offer rather than show it at a loss.

What is on your screen, and what is not

Your browser only ever receives the final price. Supplier cost, the margin and the FX rate stay on the server and appear only on the order record used for reconciliation. That is why you will never see a price change because you resized the window, and why a competitor cannot scrape our cost base.

The four steps of a booking

  1. Search. Type it or speak it. We require origin, destination and a date before searching, which keeps the search-to-booking ratio sane and the results relevant.
  2. Compare. Our rows show the fee inside the price. Partner rows show their live price with nothing added.
  3. Confirm. A person re-prices, checks baggage and change rules, and sends a secure payment link. You approve before paying.
  4. Fulfil. PNR or voucher by email, then changes, cancellations and refunds through the same team.

Questions

Is the 20% charged on top of the displayed price?
No. It is already inside the price you see. The number on the row is what you pay, assuming the supplier fare has not moved.
Why do partner prices show no markup?
Because you pay the partner, not us. Adding a fee to a price you will not pay us would be misleading, so we show their price as-is and earn a commission from them.
Can I see the supplier cost?
No, and no honest reseller shows it — it is commercially confidential. What we do publish is the exact formula and the fee percentage, so the price is auditable even if the cost is not.
What if the price changes between search and payment?
We re-price at checkout. If it moved, you see the new total and must confirm; if you decline, nothing is charged and any authorisation is released.
