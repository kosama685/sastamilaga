About us, and who the seller is

Who operates this site, which suppliers we use, what we are licensed to do, and what we deliberately do not do.

Who you are dealing with

Sasta Milaga Travel is the travel desk of sastamilaga.com, operated from Pakistan and serving travellers in Pakistan, Saudi Arabia and the Gulf. We are a travel agency and comparison desk: we quote, we book through accredited suppliers, and we support you after the sale.

What we sell, and how

Where we hold a supplier relationship, we are the seller and one 20% service fee is inside the price. Where the best price belongs to a partner — Booking.com, Agoda, Viator, DiscoverCars, Airalo and similar — we send you to them and earn a commission, with no fee added to their price.

What we are not

  • We are not a government visa portal, and we do not issue visas.
  • We are not a licensed Umrah package operator; that visa work is done by a licensed partner through Nusuk.
  • We do not scrape competitor sites in production, and we do not resell data we have no right to resell.
  • We do not claim a supplier connection we do not have. Each service page lists what is live and what is not.

Licensing and compliance

Before taking live bookings we confirm the travel and tourism services licence required in the jurisdiction we operate in, and publish the licence number in the footer once issued. Payment is taken through a licensed gateway in the business country. Where ticketing runs through an accredited supplier, the ticket is issued against that supplier's accreditation.

Questions

Who issues my ticket?
The accredited supplier we book through issues it against their accreditation; your PNR arrives by email and the airline's own site will show the booking.
Are you a licensed travel agency?
We operate as a travel agency and confirm the local travel and tourism services licence before taking live bookings, publishing the number in the footer once issued. Ask us for the current status and we will send it.
Do you resell scraped prices?
No. Production pricing comes from licensed APIs and official partner links only. Scraping competitor sites is not part of how this site works.
