Frequently asked questions

Answers on pricing, the 20% service fee, cancelled flights, Umrah visas, hotel holds, refunds and how to contact a person.

Everything below applies to every service on this site. If your question is not here, WhatsApp us — a person answers during 9 AM to 9 PM Pakistan time, Monday to Saturday.

Pricing and fees

One 20% service fee on our own fares and rates, already inside the displayed price. Zero markup on partner prices, where you pay the partner directly.

Changes, cancellations and refunds

Airline rules decide most of this, not us. Before payment we tell you the fare's change and cancellation terms, you accept them, and we record that acceptance. Refund timelines are in the refund policy.

Umrah and visas

We arrange hotels, transfers and Ziyarat. Umrah visas are issued through a licensed operator on Nusuk, because only a Ministry of Hajj and Umrah licensed company may sell those packages. Tourist visas are applied for on official government portals and we review your documents first.

Data and privacy

Passport data is encrypted, access is logged, and retention is limited to what the booking or application needs. Details in the privacy policy.

Questions

Are the prices on this page live?
Every row says where it came from. Partner rows open the partner site with that partner's price. Our own fare rows are labelled DEMO until a supplier key is connected, and no DEMO fare can be booked. Whatever you select, our team re-checks the fare before any money is taken.
How much do you add on top of the airline fare?
A flat 20% service fee, shown inside the price you see. We never display a low fare and add a surprise charge at the end. If the fee would push our margin below the cost of taking payment, we hide that fare instead of showing a worse deal.
Can I book a multi-city trip?
Yes, through our team. Send the legs on WhatsApp and we price them together. Self-serve multi-city is in progress and marked Soon on the tab.
Do you issue the ticket?
When a booking runs through our accredited supplier, the ticket is issued against their accreditation and your PNR arrives by email. Where the fare is only available on a partner site, you purchase from that partner and they issue the ticket.
What if the fare changes after I pay?
We re-price before charging. If the supplier price moved, we show the new total and ask you to confirm before any card is charged, and you can cancel for a full refund at that point.
Do you issue Umrah visas?
No. Only a Ministry of Hajj and Umrah licensed company can sell packages and process Umrah visas through Nusuk Masar. We arrange hotels, transport and guides, and work alongside a licensed operator for the visa. Anyone promising you a visa outside that system is a risk to your money.
How close can you get us to the Haram?
We quote specific properties with the distance in metres and whether it is a walkable route, and we tell you when a 'Haram view' is partial or from a corridor window.
When should I book for Ramadan?
Three to six months ahead for the last ten nights. Rates in the final third of Ramadan are the highest of the year and the good walking-distance stock goes first.
Can you handle a group of 20?
Yes. Group blocks need a deposit and a rooming list; we quote per person sharing and give you the single-supplement figure separately.
Can you guarantee a visa?
No, and no honest agent can. The decision belongs to the issuing authority. What we do is make sure your file is complete and consistent so it is not refused on a fixable technicality.
Is the Saudi eVisa the same as an Umrah visa?
No. A tourist eVisa allows tourism and, for eligible nationalities, Umrah outside Hajj season with the correct permit. A dedicated Umrah visa is issued through a licensed operator on Nusuk. We tell you which one your trip actually needs.
How long does a UAE tourist visa take?
Usually three to five working days through approved channels, longer in peak season. We quote the current timeline when you apply, not a marketing number.
Do you keep my passport scan?
Only as long as the application needs it, encrypted, with access logged, and deleted on the retention date in our privacy policy.
