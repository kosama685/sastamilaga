How the price you see is built

Supplier cost in its own currency, converted at a stored FX rate, then our fee, then rounding up to a whole unit. On flights the fee is 20%. Partner rows are exempt: you pay the partner directly at their price and we take a commission, so adding a fee there would be dishonest.

If a fare cannot clear our minimum margin after payment processing, we hide it rather than show a price we cannot honour. Full detail: how pricing works.

Flights questions

Are the prices on this page live?
Every row says where it came from. Partner rows open the partner site with that partner's price. Our own fare rows are labelled DEMO until a supplier key is connected, and no DEMO fare can be booked. Whatever you select, our team re-checks the fare before any money is taken.
How much do you add on top of the airline fare?
A flat 20% service fee, shown inside the price you see. We never display a low fare and add a surprise charge at the end. If the fee would push our margin below the cost of taking payment, we hide that fare instead of showing a worse deal.
Can I book a multi-city trip?
Yes, through our team. Send the legs on WhatsApp and we price them together. Self-serve multi-city is in progress and marked Soon on the tab.
Do you issue the ticket?
When a booking runs through our accredited supplier, the ticket is issued against their accreditation and your PNR arrives by email. Where the fare is only available on a partner site, you purchase from that partner and they issue the ticket.
What if the fare changes after I pay?
We re-price before charging. If the supplier price moved, we show the new total and ask you to confirm before any card is charged, and you can cancel for a full refund at that point.
