Where the 20% sits — and where it does not

Two different transactions, two different price rules. This is the whole pricing model, with no small print.

Partner booking

  • You pay the partner's price, exactly as shown on their site.
  • We add nothing to it. Not a cent.
  • We may earn a referral commission from the partner; it never raises your price.
  • Cancellation, changes and refunds follow the partner's terms.

Our own fare

  • Supplier net price, then one 20% service fee.
  • Calculated on our server, stored on the order, printed on your quote.
  • Re-priced before payment — if the net moved, we show you and you decide.
  • Support, changes and refunds handled by us, not a call centre abroad.

What we will not do to get you a price

This is deliberate, and it is why some rows say "open live search" instead of a number.

  • ✕

    No scraping

    We do not scrape OTA pages. Scraped fares are stale within hours, break weekly, and mislead you at checkout.

  • ✕

    No invented prices

    If we did not receive a number from a supplier API, we do not print one. A link beats a guess.

  • ✕

    No hidden fee

    One fee, one place, before payment. No resort fee surprises added by us at the last step.

Questions people ask before they compare

Is this a real price comparison?

Yes, in two honest forms. Partner rows open that partner's own live search for your route and dates, so the price is theirs and we never restate or inflate it. Our own bookable fares come from a supplier API with one 20% service fee added, itemised on the quote.

Why can't you show every website's price on this page?

Live prices need each supplier's licensed API, and every one requires a registered account. Amadeus closed self-service on 17 July 2026, Kiwi Tequila is invite-only, Skyscanner requires around 100,000 monthly users, and Hotellook shut down in October 2025. Anything claiming otherwise is scraping — stale, fragile and a legal risk. We link out instead.

What exactly is the 20% service fee?

On our own fares: supplier net plus 20%. A 230.00 net fare becomes 276.00, of which 46.00 is the fee. It is computed on our server, stored on the order and shown on your quote before payment. Partner bookings carry no fee from us.

Do I pay more if I book through a partner link?

No. The partner charges their own price, exactly as if you had typed their address. We may earn a small commission for the referral, and it never increases what you pay.

Do I need an account?

No account, no email wall, no card to compare. Either finish on the partner site, or send the trip to us on WhatsApp and we confirm the fare in writing first.

Which sites do you compare?

Flights: Google Flights, Skyscanner, Kiwi.com, Momondo. Stays: Booking.com, Agoda, Trivago, Google Hotels, Expedia, Hotels.com. Cars: DiscoverCars, Rentalcars, Kayak. Tours: Viator, GetYourGuide, Klook. eSIM: Airalo, Holafly, Saily.
