Madinah questions

When is the cheapest time to fly to Madinah?
Year-round is the sweet spot for weather. For price, avoid school holidays and the two weeks before Eid; midweek departures from Karachi, Lahore or Islamabad are consistently cheaper than Friday and Sunday.
How long is the flight to Madinah?
Direct services run from Pakistan to Madinah where the route exists; otherwise one stop in the Gulf adds two to five hours. The results page shows the duration and stop count on every fare row so you can compare like for like.
What should I budget for hotels in Madinah?
Indicative room rates start around $167 a night for a good mid-range property, service fee included. Madinah pricing swings hard around events and peak season, so a firm quote is always date-specific.
Do I need a visa for Madinah?
Visa rules depend on your passport. We link the official portal for Saudi Arabia on our visa page and check your documents before you submit — see visa help.
