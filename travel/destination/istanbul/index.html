Istanbul questions

When is the cheapest time to fly to Istanbul?
April to June, September to November is the sweet spot for weather. For price, avoid school holidays and the two weeks before Eid; midweek departures from Karachi, Lahore or Islamabad are consistently cheaper than Friday and Sunday.
How long is the flight to Istanbul?
Direct services run from Pakistan to Istanbul where the route exists; otherwise one stop in the Gulf adds two to five hours. The results page shows the duration and stop count on every fare row so you can compare like for like.
What should I budget for hotels in Istanbul?
Indicative room rates start around $183 a night for a good mid-range property, service fee included. Istanbul pricing swings hard around events and peak season, so a firm quote is always date-specific.
Do I need a visa for Istanbul?
Visa rules depend on your passport. We link the official portal for Turkey on our visa page and check your documents before you submit — see visa help.
Can you arrange history in Istanbul?
Yes — history, Bosphorus cruises, shopping and food are all quotable. Send the dates and traveller count through the request form and we return a written plan with the fee included.
