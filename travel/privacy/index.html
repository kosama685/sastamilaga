Privacy policy

What we collect, why, how long we keep it, and how passport and payment data are handled.

What we collect

  • Search data: route, dates, travellers. Used to return results and to improve the site.
  • Contact data: name and WhatsApp number, when you ask for a quote.
  • Booking data: passenger names as printed on passports, dates of birth where a supplier requires them, and contact details.
  • Payment data: handled entirely by the licensed gateway. We never see or store full card numbers.

Passport and identity data

Encrypted at rest, access logged, retained only as long as the booking or visa application requires, then deleted on the retention date. We do not sell it and we share it only with the supplier fulfilling your booking, or an authority where the law requires it.

Cookies

Essential cookies keep the site working. Analytics, where enabled, is aggregated and does not identify you individually. No advertising cookies are set without consent.

Your rights

Ask for a copy of your data, correction, or deletion by messaging WhatsApp. We respond within 30 days. Where a booking is live, some data must be retained until travel completes for legal and supplier reasons.

Third parties

Partner links (Booking.com, Agoda, Viator, DiscoverCars, Airalo and others) have their own privacy policies once you leave this site. Payment is processed by a licensed gateway in the business country.

Questions

Do you sell my data?
No. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we share it only with the supplier fulfilling your booking.
How long do you keep passport scans?
Only for the period the booking or application needs, then deleted on a set retention date. You can ask us to confirm the date for your file.
