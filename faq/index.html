Help centre & FAQ

Everything shoppers ask, answered plainly: ordering, cash on delivery, delivery times, returns, refunds, sourcing and privacy.

Ordering

Add items to the basket, open the basket, fill in name, phone, city and address, then send the order summary on WhatsApp. We confirm stock and dispatch. No account and no card required.

Cash on delivery

Available from Rs 199 to Rs 15,000 in Pakistan and across the COD markets listed on the channels page. Inspect before paying. Refuse if it is wrong.

Delivery

2–5 working days inside Pakistan; 4–8 days in the Gulf; 7–18 days worldwide. Supplier-direct items are labelled Global shipping and take 10–25 days.

Returns

14 days, unused, original packaging. Faulty or wrong items are returned free. See returns & refunds.

Sourcing

If a product is not listed, send a photo or link on WhatsApp. We quote price, delivery time and COD eligibility before you commit.

Questions about this page

Is cash on delivery really available?

Yes, in every market listed in our COD matrix — Pakistan, the Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, India, Bangladesh, Southeast Asia, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Southern and Eastern Europe, Latin America and parts of Africa. Where no courier collects cash, checkout shows prepaid only instead of pretending COD exists.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free on orders over Rs 2,500. Below that we charge a flat Rs 199 inside Pakistan; other countries are priced at checkout by the courier. The amount is shown before you confirm.

Can I check the parcel before paying?

Yes. Open the parcel in front of the courier and check the item, size and quantity before you hand over the cash. If it is not what you ordered, refuse the parcel and you pay nothing.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

Send a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. We arrange a replacement or a refund to your wallet or bank account, and we cover the return shipping when the fault is ours.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are approved within 2 working days of the returned parcel reaching us and are paid within 5–7 working days to your bank account, JazzCash or Easypaisa.

Do you ship products from China?

Some products come from our supplier network and are labelled Global shipping. Those take 10–25 days, are prepaid only, and are never offered as cash on delivery because no courier will collect cash for them.

Is my phone number safe?

We use it to confirm the order and for the courier to call you. We do not sell customer data. Our checkout runs on this site and the order summary opens in your own WhatsApp — no card details ever touch our servers.

Do you have a physical address?

Orders and support run through WhatsApp on +966 51 045 1995 and our contact page. We publish our returns address on every parcel label so a return always has somewhere to go.

Do you offer wholesale pricing?

Yes for resellers and offices. Message with “BULK” and the quantity for tiered pricing.

Can I order for someone else?

Yes. Put their name, phone and address at checkout so the courier calls the right person.

Still need a human?

Message us on WhatsApp — order status, sizes, returns, bulk pricing or sourcing a product that is not listed.
