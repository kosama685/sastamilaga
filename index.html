How a cash-on-delivery order works

Four steps, no card details, no advance.

STEP 01

Pick and add to basket

Browse a category or search the catalogue. Items marked COD can be paid in cash.

STEP 02

Give a phone number and address

We only need your name, phone, city and street address. The courier calls before arriving.

STEP 03

Your basket becomes a WhatsApp message with the items and total. Send it and we confirm stock.

Open the parcel in front of the rider. Check it. Then hand over the cash — or refuse it and pay nothing.

Is cash on delivery really available?

Yes, in every market listed in our COD matrix — Pakistan, the Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, India, Bangladesh, Southeast Asia, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Southern and Eastern Europe, Latin America and parts of Africa. Where no courier collects cash, checkout shows prepaid only instead of pretending COD exists.

How much is delivery?

Delivery is free on orders over Rs 2,500. Below that we charge a flat Rs 199 inside Pakistan; other countries are priced at checkout by the courier. The amount is shown before you confirm.

Can I check the parcel before paying?

Yes. Open the parcel in front of the courier and check the item, size and quantity before you hand over the cash. If it is not what you ordered, refuse the parcel and you pay nothing.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

Send a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery. We arrange a replacement or a refund to your wallet or bank account, and we cover the return shipping when the fault is ours.

How long does a refund take?

Refunds are approved within 2 working days of the returned parcel reaching us and are paid within 5–7 working days to your bank account, JazzCash or Easypaisa.

Do you ship products from China?

Some products come from our supplier network and are labelled Global shipping. Those take 10–25 days, are prepaid only, and are never offered as cash on delivery because no courier will collect cash for them.

Not sure which size, model or colour?

Send a photo or a link on WhatsApp. We check stock with the supplier and reply with the exact price, delivery date and COD eligibility before you order.
