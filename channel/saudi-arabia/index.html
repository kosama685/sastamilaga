Saudi Arabia — FAQ

Do you deliver to Saudi Arabia?

Yes — delivery across ksa with cash on delivery in major cities and prepaid options everywhere else. Deliveries run through SMSA, Aramex, Naqel in about 4–8 working days.

Is cash on delivery available?

Yes, cash on delivery is available for this channel. Prices are shown in SAR as an indicative conversion. The courier collects the exact amount printed on the parcel.

What is the delivery charge?

Free over Rs 2,500 inside Pakistan, otherwise a flat Rs 199. For Saudi Arabia the courier rate is calculated at checkout by weight and destination.

How do I track my order?

You get a tracking number on WhatsApp when the parcel is handed to the courier. Our track-order page also explains how to check the status.

What about customs and taxes?

Prices include local tax where applicable. Import duty or VAT charged at the border is separate and collected by the courier, not by us.
