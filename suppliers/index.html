How our sourcing works

Where products come from, how they are selected, and the rules that keep the catalogue honest.

Three sources

  • Local stock — held in Pakistan for fast cash-on-delivery dispatch.
  • CJ Dropshipping — global catalogue, 10–25 day delivery, prepaid.
  • Regional suppliers — EU and Gulf warehouses used when a product ships faster from a local hub.

Selection rules

A product is published only if it has a real image, a real price, a stated weight and a delivery route to at least one market. That is why thousands of raw supplier records never reach the site: no image, no price, or no way to deliver means no page.

Quality control

  • Photograph every local-stock parcel before handover.
  • Sample anything before scaling it.
  • Track return rate per product; a product with a high return rate is removed.
  • No counterfeit or restricted items, ever.

Honest delivery labels

Every product page states Local stock or Global shipping, with the realistic delivery window. We do not advertise 2–5 days on a parcel that ships from China.

Questions about this page

Why are some products cheaper?

Supplier-direct items skip local warehousing, so they cost less but take longer. Local stock costs more and arrives in days.

Are branded products genuine?

Only products with verifiable supplier authenticity are listed. If we cannot verify it, it is not sold.

Can a product disappear?

Yes. Supplier stock moves. If an item vanishes after you order, we tell you and offer a substitute or cancellation.
